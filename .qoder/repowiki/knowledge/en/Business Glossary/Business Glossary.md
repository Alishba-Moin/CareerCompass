### CareerCompass
- Definition：Internal product name for the AI-powered career guidance platform built for Pakistani Intermediate and University Graduate students. The prototype centers on one student, one AI Career Coach, and one structured career roadmap rather than a generic chatbot.
- Aliases：CC

### Multi-Agent Pipeline
- Definition：Architectural concept of the product: six sub-agents (Career Coach orchestrator, Skill Assessment, Market Intel, Career Path, Roadmap Gen, Progress Tracker) that collaborate to evaluate skills, market data, and paths to produce a personalized roadmap. In the prototype this is a visual animation; future stages plan to implement them as real services.
- Aliases：Agents、Sub-agents

### Career Readiness Score
- Definition：A 0–100 composite badge displayed on the profile dashboard that reflects how prepared a student is for their target career path. It is animated on load and shows month-over-month change (e.g., +8).
- Aliases：Readiness Score

### Skill Gap Analysis
- Definition：Side-by-side view listing a student's current strengths versus gaps they need to close for a chosen career path, shown as color-coded progress bars (green for strengths, amber/rose for gaps).
- Aliases：Strengths vs Gaps

### Pakistan Job Market Insights
- Definition：Dashboard section showing local (Pakistan) and remote global job demand, salary ranges (PKR/month and USD/month), top hiring hubs, and freelance platforms relevant to Pakistani developers. Uses Local/Remote tab switching.
- Aliases：Market Insights、Local/Remote Market

### 4-Week Action Plan
- Definition：Personalized weekly checklist (Foundation → Build → ML Intro → Launch) with interactive checkboxes that strike through completed items. Each week has themed tasks aimed at moving the student toward job readiness.
- Aliases：Action Plan、Weekly Plan

### Portfolio Project Recommendation Card
- Definition：Suggested capstone projects (e.g., Full Stack Task Manager, ML Sentiment Analyzer, Pakistan Job Trends Dashboard) each presented as a card showing tech stack, estimated duration, and impact rating.
- Aliases：Project Cards

### Ask Career Coach
- Definition：Interactive chat area where students ask career questions in English or Roman Urdu. The prototype simulates responses from the multi-agent system; future stages intend to connect it to a real LLM backend.
- Aliases：Coach Chat、AI Coach
